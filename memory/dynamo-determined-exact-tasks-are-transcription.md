`dynamo-ce5b6ea`, 2026-08-14. Two complete tasks, nine pass@2 draws and one
pass@5, every review and QC gate green on both, and the difficulty draw never
landed an anchor.

| concept | shape | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| quayside-settle | complete normative charter, 20 of 31 misreadings blind on the shipped fixture | timeouts when heavy, 2/2 solved when light — see [[dynamo-volume-bound-tasks-oscillate]] |
| meterline-refit | tiny deliverable, rate card recovered from a ledger, 16 of 24 misreadings invisible to any local self-test | 2/2 solved at 27/46 min, then 2/2 again at 28 min with 32 to spare |

**Why:** QC fairness requires every graded rule to be stated or uniquely
derivable. Once it is, a frontier agent reads it precisely and implements it —
so the blind-branch design buys nothing, because blind branches only catch a
solver who *guesses*. Making the constants inferred does not help either: on the
last head each probe bounded a constant to a four-credit window instead of
giving it, and the trial log shows the agent writing "an explicit
4-nested-loop brute-force scan... confirming a single candidate". That is
[[dynamo-z3-collapses-joint-integer-fits]] and
[[dynamo-recovered-constants-are-still-transcription]] restated: a search the
author can prove terminates is a search the agent can run.

**How to apply:** stop treating "how hard is the computation" as the axis. For
this reference pair the only lever left in the playbook that neither concept
used is the salvage/repair mold's **operational irreversibility** — the tool
consumes the evidence it verified, so a draft run on the sole live copy destroys
graded state and the kill does not depend on misreading anything. Budget that
shape from commit 1 rather than discovering it after two rebuilds, and note the
tell: if every failure analysis says `approach_validity PASS` and
`task_specification PASS`, no amount of extra rule depth will move the draw.
